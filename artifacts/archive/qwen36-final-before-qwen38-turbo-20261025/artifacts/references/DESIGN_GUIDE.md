## Shared Design Choices

1. **Stylized-realistic game-art style:** Clean geometry with readable silhouettes. Not photorealistic but not cartoonish. Subsurface scattering on pins for a convincing appearance.
2. **Warm ambient lighting:** Light wood tones for lanes, dark gutters, white pins, with warm colored seating and scoreboards providing visual interest.
3. **Modular construction:** Pins and balls are separate reusable objects; lanes, gutters, and static geometry are separate collection members under `BowlingPair01_ROOT`.
4. **Symmetry:** Both lanes are mirror-symmetric around the origin. This simplifies construction — build one lane pair and mirror, or use identical dimensions with mirrored transforms.
