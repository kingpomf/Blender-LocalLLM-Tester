# Stage 04 — Seat and Steering

## Required objects

- `Kart01_Seat`
- `Kart01_SteeringColumn`
- `Kart01_SteeringWheel`
- `Kart01_Pedal_L`
- `Kart01_Pedal_R`

## Requirements

- Seat is centered on X = 0 in the rear half of the kart.
- Seat has a distinct base and back silhouette; one joined mesh is acceptable.
- Steering column points from the front-center chassis area toward the driver.
- Steering wheel is a simple low-poly ring or three-spoke approximation.
- Two distinct pedals sit ahead of the seat and remain above the floor.
- No component may visibly float away from or deeply intersect its support.

## Budget

All Stage 04 mesh objects combined: `<= 1,000` triangles.
