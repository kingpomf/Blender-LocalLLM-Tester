# Stage 03 — Axles and Wheels

## Required objects

- `Kart01_Axle_Front`
- `Kart01_Axle_Rear`
- `Kart01_Wheel_FL`
- `Kart01_Wheel_FR`
- `Kart01_Wheel_RL`
- `Kart01_Wheel_RR`

## Requirements

- Exactly four visible wheels.
- Wheel rotation axes run along X.
- Wheel diameter, width, wheelbase, and tracks meet Stage 01 tolerances.
- Left/right wheel pairs are symmetric around X = 0.
- Wheels do not visibly intersect chassis or floor.
- Tire tread is optional; do not model fine tread geometry.
- Each wheel may be a joined low-poly tire/rim mesh or parented tire/rim children, but the required wheel object names must exist.

## Budget

All wheel and axle mesh objects combined: `<= 1,500` triangles.
