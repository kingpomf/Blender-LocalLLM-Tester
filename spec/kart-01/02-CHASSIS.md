# Stage 02 — Chassis and Bumpers

## Required objects

- `Kart01_Root` — empty at world origin
- `Kart01_Chassis` — single low-poly main chassis/body
- `Kart01_Floor` — flat driver floor
- `Kart01_Bumper_Front`
- `Kart01_Bumper_Rear`
- `Kart01_SidePod_L`
- `Kart01_SidePod_R`

## Shape requirements

- Main chassis is centered on X = 0 and no lower than Z = 0.17 m.
- Front nose is visibly narrower than the rear body.
- Floor spans the driver foot and seat area without intersecting the ground.
- Bumpers are simple rounded rectangular or tubular forms.
- Side pods are mirrored in position and dimensions.
- Use bevels or bevel modifiers sparingly to avoid razor-sharp silhouette edges.

## Budget

All Stage 02 mesh objects combined: `<= 1,500` triangles after evaluated modifiers.
