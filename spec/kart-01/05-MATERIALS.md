# Stage 05 — Materials

## Required materials

- `MAT_Kart_Red` — chassis, bumpers, side pods; base color near `#C62828`
- `MAT_Rubber_Dark` — tires; base color near `#171717`, roughness `>= 0.65`
- `MAT_Metal_Dark` — axles, steering column, pedals
- `MAT_Rim_Silver` — visible rims
- `MAT_Seat_Black` — seat and steering wheel

## Rules

- Use Principled BSDF materials only.
- No external image textures.
- Every visible mesh must have at least one assigned material.
- Avoid duplicate materials with numeric suffixes.
- Keep the look stylized and readable; photorealism is not a goal.
