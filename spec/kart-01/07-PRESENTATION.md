# Stage 07 — Presentation Cameras and Lighting

## Required cameras

- `CAM_Kart01_Perspective`
- `CAM_Kart01_Front`
- `CAM_Kart01_Side`
- `CAM_Kart01_Rear`

Each camera must frame the entire kart with visible margin and use a neutral focal length without extreme perspective distortion.

## Required presentation objects

- `Kart01_Ground` — neutral matte plane at Z = 0
- `LIGHT_Kart01_Key`
- `LIGHT_Kart01_Fill`
- `LIGHT_Kart01_Rim`

Use neutral lighting that makes geometry easy to judge. Render `512 x 512` PNG images to:

- `artifacts/renders/Kart01_perspective.png`
- `artifacts/renders/Kart01_front.png`
- `artifacts/renders/Kart01_side.png`
- `artifacts/renders/Kart01_rear.png`
