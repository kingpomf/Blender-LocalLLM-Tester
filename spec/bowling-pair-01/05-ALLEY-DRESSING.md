# Stage 05 — Return, seating and alley dressing

Required supporting assets, each clearly named and modular:

- One shared ball-return hood and rack, centered between lane approaches. Its
  footprint must remain within X=-0.40..0.40, Y=-4.30..-0.30, so neither approach
  walking/throwing corridor is blocked. Include a return opening and rack/cradle
  geometry that visibly supports all three balls. Decorative internal machinery
  is unnecessary.
- Two benches or seat units behind the approaches (all seat geometry Y<-4.572).
- Two overhead scoreboard housings, one per lane, with screen bottoms Z>=2.20.
  Screens must have different readable lane labels `01` and `02` and a simple
  bowling-score-like grid. Static display only; no scoring logic.
- Rear masking wall above/behind the pits with `01` and `02` lane identification;
  at least 0.60 m clearance above the standing pin tops where it spans the rack.
- Alley floor under the assembly and seating; at least two original decorative
  elements (e.g. wall stripes, ceiling light bars, wall panels). Keep the front
  and one side open enough for review cameras.

Use original generic designs without external logos. Report counts, placements
and clearances. Alley dressing must read as one cohesive pair of lanes, not
unrelated props. No full building, toilets, restaurant or additional lanes needed.
