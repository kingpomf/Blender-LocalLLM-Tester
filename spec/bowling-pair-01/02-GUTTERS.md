# Stage 02 — Gutters, decks and pit

- Four independent, continuous gutters: `Gutter_L_Outer`, `Gutter_L_Inner`,
  `Gutter_R_Inner`, `Gutter_R_Outer`. Each directly borders its lane with a
  lateral gap no greater than 0.01 m and spans Y=0..19.20 (±0.01 m).
- Each gutter is 0.22–0.25 m wide, has an open concave cross-section and a trough
  bottom 0.08–0.12 m below the lane. A flat dark strip does not count as a gutter.
  The lane-facing lip must not protrude above Z=0.005.
- Include a central capping/divider between the two inner gutters, outer rails,
  a pin deck region for each rack, and a visible recessed rear pit/catch area
  beginning at Y=19.20. Pit extends at least 0.60 m farther back.
- Pin deck top is Z=0 ±0.002 and covers the entire rack footprint. No raised
  barriers cross the ball path before the head pin. Rails must not intrude onto
  the 1.0668 m lane width.

Report measured cross-section samples, longitudinal bounds, lane-edge adjacency,
deck coverage and pit depth. Gutters and pit need visible interior geometry;
their collision simplification comes in Stage 06.
